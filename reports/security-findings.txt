SECURITY FINDINGS - DAY 4

Target: 192.168.56.102

Environment: Metasploitable 2

FINDING 1: LEGACY REMOTE ACCESS SERVICES

Affected Ports:
23/tcp - Telnet
512/tcp - rexec
513/tcp - rlogin
514/tcp - rsh

Risk:
These legacy protocols may expose sensitive
communication because they lack modern
transport security.

Severity: High (provisional)

Recommendation:
Disable unnecessary legacy services.
Use SSH for secure remote administration.

FINDING 2: FTP SERVICE

Port: 21/tcp
Detected Version: vsftpd 2.3.4

Risk:
Traditional FTP does not encrypt credentials
or transferred data.

Additional Note:
The detected version has historically been
associated with a serious backdoor issue.
The installed instance has not been verified
as vulnerable.

Severity: Medium (provisional)

Recommendation:
Replace FTP with SFTP or securely configured
FTPS where appropriate.

ASSESSMENT LIMITATION:
These findings are based on remote service
identification. Exploitability has not been
tested or confirmed.
